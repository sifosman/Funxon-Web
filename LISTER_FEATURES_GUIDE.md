- **Paid (Monthly/6Mo/12Mo):** Everything in Free PLUS catalogue, portfolio manager, analytics, quote requests, website/social links, and instant tour bookings.

- **Premium:** Everything in Free PLUS 40 photos, 5 videos, full catalogue, reviews, analytics, WhatsApp, website/social links.
- **Premium Plus:** Everything in Premium PLUS 60 photos, 10 videos, and a **Dedicated Portfolio Manager**.
