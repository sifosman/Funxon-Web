Complete Feature Directory

Every tool and feature available to listers on the Funxon platform.

A Profile & Portfolio Management

  • 1. Portfolio CreationApply to create a new venue or vendor portfolio via the step-by-step application flow.All Plans
  • 2. Portfolio Edit / UpdateEdit your business name, description, address, location, contact details, and tags at any time.All Plans
  • 3. Service Options & TagsSelect relevant categories, amenities, and tags so clients can find you in search.All Plans
  • 4. Price Range DisplayShow a price indicator (e.g. R, RR, RRR) on your public profile.All Plans
  • 5. Map Location DisplayYour address is geocoded and displayed on an interactive map on your public profile.All Plans
  • 6. Website & Social LinksAdd your website URL, Instagram, and other social media links.Paid Plans Only
  • 7. Public Profile ScreenA dedicated public-facing profile page with gallery, contact buttons, and catalogue access.All Plans
  • 8. Image ZoomUsers can tap photos on your profile to view them full-screen.All Plans
  • 9. Share ProfileUsers can share your venue/vendor profile via native share sheet.All Plans
  • 10. FavouritesUsers can favourite your listing and find it later in their saved list.All Plans

B Media & Content

  • 11. Photo UploadsUpload high-quality images to your portfolio gallery.Varies by Plan
  • 12. Video UploadsUpload promotional or showcase videos.Varies by Plan
  • 13. Photo Upload CounterVisual indicator showing how many photos you have used vs. your plan limit.All Plans
  • 14. PDF Document UploadUpload pricelists, menus, or brochures as PDF documents to your catalogue.Paid Plans

C Business Catalogue & Pricing

  • 15. Catalogue ManagementAdd, edit, and remove items from your service/venue catalogue with titles, descriptions, prices, and images.Venue: Paid OnlyVendor: Free up to 10
  • 16. Real-Time Price TotalsCatalogue screen calculates running totals as you add or update item prices.All Plans
  • 17. PDF Catalogue FallbackIf you prefer, upload a PDF pricelist instead of building an itemised catalogue.Paid Plans
  • 18. View Public CatalogueUsers browsing your profile can tap "View Catalogue" to see your offerings.All Plans

D Communication & Client Engagement

  • 19. Live WhatsApp ChatOne-tap WhatsApp button on your public profile so browsers can message you directly.Venue: All PlansVendor: Paid Only
  • 20. Online Quote RequestsClients submit quote enquiries directly through your profile; you receive them in-app.Venue: Paid OnlyVendor: All Plans
  • 21. Quote Request ManagementView incoming enquiries, see client details/event info, and update status.Venue: Paid OnlyVendor: All Plans
  • 22. Create & Send QuotesBuild and send formal quotes back to clients with line items, prices, and totals.All Plans
  • 23. Quote History & RevisionsTrack all quotes sent for a request, view revision history, and monitor status.All Plans
  • 24. Ratings & ReviewsClients leave star ratings and written reviews on your profile after an event.Venue: All PlansVendor: Paid Only
  • 25. Review DisplayYour average rating and review count appear on your profile and in search results.All Plans

E Calendar & Availability

  • 26. Calendar Availability UpdatesMark dates as available or busy so clients know when you can be booked.All Plans
  • 27. Tour Booking CalendarVenues can accept and manage instant tour booking requests with preferred dates/times.Paid Plans

F Venue-Specific Features

  • 28. Instant Venue Tour BookingsClients request a site visit/tour directly from your venue profile.Paid Plans
  • 29. Tour Booking ManagementApprove, schedule, or decline tour requests; update status.Paid Plans
  • 30. Venue AnalyticsBasic in-app analytics showing counts of catalogue items, quote requests, and tour bookings.Paid Plans

G Vendor-Specific Features

  • 31. Vendor DashboardOverview screen showing your rating, recent reviews, and pending quote requests.All Plans
  • 32. Quote Request AlertsReal-time badge/notification showing how many pending quote requests you have.All Plans

H Billing & Subscription

  • 33. Plan Selection & CheckoutBrowse plans, select billing period (monthly/yearly), and subscribe via PayFast.All Users
  • 34. Billing DashboardView current subscription tier, status, next payment due, and billing details.Active Subscribers
  • 35. Invoice HistoryView and track all past invoices with amounts, periods, and payment status.Active Subscribers
  • 36. Upgrade / DowngradeChange your plan or billing period at any time.All Subscribers

I Support & Account Management

  • 37. Portfolio Build AssistanceAccess help and guidance for creating and optimising your portfolio.All Plans
  • 38. Full-Time Helpdesk SupportIn-app help centre and support access.All Plans
  • 39. Dedicated Portfolio ManagerA Funxon representative personally assists with your portfolio.Premium Plus & Venue Paid
  • 40. Account SettingsUpdate email, password, display name, and marketing preferences.All Users

Quick Reference: Who Gets What

A simple summary of what is included in each lister type.

Venue Lister

  • Free: Basic profile, 10 photos, 1 video, calendar, map, WhatsApp, reviews, helpdesk.
  • Paid: Everything in Free PLUS catalogue, portfolio manager, analytics, quote requests, website/social links, and instant tour bookings.

Vendor / Service Professional

  • Free: Basic profile, 10 photos, up to 10 catalogue items, calendar, quote requests, helpdesk, portfolio editing.
  • Premium: Everything in Free PLUS 40 photos, 5 videos, full catalogue, reviews, analytics, WhatsApp, website/social links.
  • Premium Plus: Everything in Premium PLUS 60 photos, 10 videos, and a Dedicated Portfolio Manager.

Document generated for client reference. Features reflect the current state of the Funxon mobile application as of June 2026.
